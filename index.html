FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this an official Microsoft extension?

No. CoPilot EveryWhere - SidePanel is an independent extension and is not affiliated with Microsoft. It opens the Copilot website in Chrome's side panel.

Does it read the pages I visit?

No. The extension never reads or sends the content of the pages you browse. To ask about a page, copy the text into Copilot yourself.

Do I need an account?

You can start chatting right away. Signing in keeps your chat history.

Why does it ask for access to copilot.com?

That permission lets Copilot load inside the side panel. Chrome asks once, the first time you open the panel. Depending on your region, Copilot may redirect to copilot.microsoft.com, so that site is included too.

Why does sign-in open in a new tab?

Sign-in pages refuse to run inside other pages for security reasons. The extension opens sign-in in a normal tab and closes it automatically when you're done.

How do I change the keyboard shortcut?

Open chrome://extensions/shortcuts or click "Change" next to the shortcut in the extension's Settings.

Which browsers are supported?

Google Chrome 116 and newer. Other Chromium browsers that support Chrome's side panel should work too.
